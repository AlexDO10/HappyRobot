## The opportunity

Every inbound carrier call is both a margin opportunity and a time cost. Today,
reps spend a large share of their day on calls that never book; after-hours and
overflow calls go unanswered; and under pressure to move a load, even good reps
sometimes give away margin.

We built an AI voice agent that answers your inbound carrier calls automatically,
end to end — capturing more calls, protecting your margin with consistent
rules-based negotiation, and freeing your reps to focus on closing.

---

## What it does

When a carrier calls in, the agent handles the entire first conversation:

1. **Greets** the carrier and asks for their MC number.
2. **Verifies** they are authorized to haul, against the official FMCSA registry.
3. **Finds** a load that matches their lane and equipment from your board.
4. **Negotiates** the rate — within limits you set — and books the load.
5. **Transfers** the booked carrier to one of your reps to finalize.
6. **Logs** the outcome of every call to a live dashboard.

It runs 24/7, handles many calls at once, and never deviates from your pricing
rules.

---

## Why it protects your margin

This is the part that matters most: **the AI never knows or reveals your
walk-away price.** Your maximum buy rate — your confidential ceiling — stays
locked inside the system. The carrier on the phone can never see it, and the AI
cannot be talked into exceeding it.

Instead, the agent follows a negotiation strategy you control:

- It **opens at your posted rate.**
- It **concedes gradually** across up to three rounds — smaller steps each round.
- It will **never agree above your ceiling.** If the carrier won't come down, it
  politely walks away rather than overpaying.

Pricing is governed by fixed business rules, not by the AI's judgment in the
moment. That means consistent margins on every call — no rep giving away rates
under pressure, and no AI improvising a number.

## What you see — the live dashboard

A web dashboard, open from any browser, updated in real time:

- **Volume & booking rate** — how many carriers called and how many booked.
- **Conversion funnel** — calls → eligible → load matched → booked.
- **Negotiation effectiveness** — how close to your posted rate you're booking,
  and how much margin you're keeping below your ceiling, in dollars.
- **Carrier sentiment** — the tone of your carrier interactions.
- **Recent calls** — a searchable log of every call and its outcome.

You get a clear, quantified picture of exactly how your carrier desk is
performing — something that's hard to measure today.

## Trust & safety

- **Carrier authorization is verified** against the official FMCSA registry
  before any load is discussed — you only do business with eligible carriers.
- **Your pricing limits are confidential** and never exposed — not to the
  carrier, not even within the AI's conversation.
- **A human rep finalizes every booking** — the agent hands off, it doesn't
  replace your team.
- **Securely hosted** with encrypted connections throughout.

## What's needed to run it

- Your **load list** — lanes, equipment, pickup/delivery windows, your posted
  rate, and your confidential walk-away rate per load.
- That's it. The system is **hosted and managed** — there's nothing for your team
  to install or maintain.
